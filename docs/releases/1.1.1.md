# Spine Contour 1.1.1

This release moves the level editor's name plates out of the way: each one now sits outside its vertebra, and any
plate can be dragged.

## The level plates

- In edit mode, with the overlays on, every placed level but a femoral head carries a small plate with its name. Each
  plate now sits **outside its level, on the posterior side**, 14 px beside the posterior edge (its midpoint for a
  body, the posterior corner for C2 and the endplates), whichever way the film faces, and it stays wholly on the film
  however far it is dragged. Before, every plate went to the right of the posterior edge, which on a film facing right put it inside the
  vertebral body, over the anatomy being checked.
- Each level reads its side from its own points: a body from its posterior edge against its middle, C2 from its
  inferior-posterior corner against its centroid, an endplate (T1, S1) from its posterior corner against its
  anterior one. Where a level cannot say, as for T4's single point, the film's anterior side decides and the plate
  goes the other way.
- **Drag a plate** to move it out of the way: the cursor shows grab over a plate and grabbing during the drag, and
  the plate follows the pointer at any zoom. A **double-click** on a plate puts it back. The selected level's handles
  are still grabbed first where a plate lies over one; plates do not move while placing a level, with the window /
  level panel open or with Pan pressed, and a right-button drag on a plate still windows the film.
- Where the plates were dragged is part of the view, like zoom, pan and the window: it lasts through leaving and
  entering edit mode, resets when another film is opened, and is never saved. Moving a plate changes no level, no
  measurement and no label record.

## Verification

974 desktop tests (953 in 1.1.0; the new ones cover where each plate sits for a film facing either way, the drag's
offsets, the plates the stage hit-tests, and the view's reset). The level editor's CDP smoke suite, phase `dom`, read
121/121 on fresh scratch profiles, three times, by DOM reads only, its new section checking on a synthetic film that
every plate lies outside its level on the posterior side, that a drag moves a plate by the pointer's travel, that a
handle under a plate still wins, that a double-click puts the plate back and that opening another film resets the
plates. The suite's film phase then read 25/25 on one copied real film, and on a second fresh launch the plan-04
suites read parity 15/15, gate1 25/25, gate2 26/26 (handle drags still win over plates), gate3 26/26, chip 20/20 and
chord 28/28, all by DOM reads only. The backend is unchanged from 1.1.0, so its suite was not re-run, nor were the
other smoke suites. These are software and integration checks, not clinical validation. The
release workflow builds and tests the Windows and macOS installers before publication; merging the release PR to
`main` publishes v1.1.1.

## Compatibility

The store format, the label record and its schema are unchanged. The macOS build remains unsigned.
