# Spine Contour 1.0.7

Femoral heads now display as circles, centre marks and a diamond at the midpoint
between the two centres. Raw femoral masks remain internal to automatic fitting
and are never drawn in the analysis or comparison viewer, including older studies.

Choose **Edit landmarks** to move a circle by its centre or resize it at its rim.
Select a circle and use **Delete circle**, Delete or Backspace to remove it.
**Add circle** and **Retrace** accept at least three points around the head; use
**Fit** to apply them. Escape cancels the unfinished trace. At most two circles
are supported; Head 1/2 are editing identifiers, not left/right anatomical labels.

Edits are saved after measurements recalculate. With fewer than two circles, the
hip midpoint, PI, PT and L1PA are unavailable. Other supported measurements and
calibrated disc heights remain available. Reset to prediction restores the original
geometry, measurements and quality checks. Missing heads can be added manually
without rerunning the models.

The **Overall confidence** badge reads *Review recommended*, *Limited information*
or *Checks passed* — deliberately none of the words the status badge beside it
uses — and expands to show why. It includes partial anatomy, orientation,
individual S1/crop/femoral scores, measurement consistency and image calibration.
This is a quality-check assessment, not a validated probability of correct
measurements: the vertebral model provides no calibrated confidence score.
Original scores remain identified as original after manual edits.

This release also includes the 1.0.6 studies-table changes that shipped from main
while this work was in progress: deleting the ticked studies, sortable Find headers,
in-place SUBJECT editing, the Reviewed status and the development-only demo switch.

Merging this version into main invokes the existing Windows/macOS release pipeline.
The release is published only after both installer builds succeed. No model weights,
resolution, inference runtime, dependencies or calibration algorithms change.
