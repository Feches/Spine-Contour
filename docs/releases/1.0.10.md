# Spine Contour 1.0.10

Cervical and standing full-spine films can now be measured alongside lumbar ones, and new
films no longer need their region chosen by hand. A cervical landmark model reports the
C2–C7 Cobb angle and the C2–C7 sagittal vertical axis. A standing film returns the global
C7–S1 sagittal vertical axis together with the cervical, lumbar and pelvic measurements
available on it. **Auto detect** reads the film to choose between the three. Each
construction is drawn on the film and its landmarks can be corrected. This release
includes 1.0.9's femoral-head model unchanged.

## Automatic film detection

New imports and new workspace folders start on **Auto detect**. It runs the existing
cervical and lumbar crop searches and picks cervical, lumbar or full-spine processing from
the anatomy it finds, not from the file name or the image's shape. Studies already in the
library keep the region they had. Choose **Lumbar**, **Cervical · HRNET** or **Full spine ·
HRNET** on the study or its workspace folder to override the choice. When the evidence
conflicts or is missing, the app asks for a region instead of guessing, and no result is
saved.

On a standing film the anterior side is detected too. Both horizontal orientations are
checked for a consistent lumbar chain and the anatomical order of the S1 endpoints; a tie
or no evidence asks for **Anterior is image left** or **right**, and a chosen side always
wins. A cervical-only film still needs its anterior side chosen, because the cervical
model's labels do not show which way the patient faces; Auto detect asks for it and a
rerun. The film must be an upright lateral radiograph: Auto detect does not correct
rotation or recognise an AP projection.

Detection adds crop searches, so an Auto run takes longer than one with the region and
side chosen. Choosing the region explicitly skips detection. Progress, cancellation and the
low-memory setting work as before. `docs/automatic-film-detection.md` describes the checks.

## Measuring a cervical film

Choose whether anterior is on the image's left or right. The original film is shown
first, DICOM included, so the side can be read before anything runs. The side decides
which corner of C7 is posterior.

Run the model and review the landmarks on the source image. Selecting a measurement row
draws its construction. The six measurement handles can be dragged: the values recalculate
and the corrected points are saved with the study, and **Reset** restores the prediction.
A missing landmark leaves the values that depend on it empty instead of guessing, and a
missed spine detection does not fall back to a whole-image guess.

- **C2–C7 Cobb** is the unsigned acute angle between the C2 and C7 inferior endplates. It
  does not use the C7 superior endplate and carries no lordosis or kyphosis sign.
- **C2–C7 SVA** is the horizontal distance from the C2 body centroid to the C7
  superior-posterior corner, positive when C2 lies anterior on the selected side. The
  plumb line follows the image's vertical; the film is not rotated upright.

Check that the vertebra marked C2 is C2, that C7 is fully on the film and that each corner
a construction uses sits on visible cortex before accepting the numbers.
`docs/cervical-alignment.md` describes the model, its training data and the development
checks.

## Standing films and global C7–S1 SVA

A standing film is searched with the cervical models in its upper part and with the S1
localizer and lumbar HRNET in its lower part, over overlapping crops. A landmark is kept
only when the crops agree on it; a missing or disputed one is withheld for review. The
result carries global C7–S1 SVA and, where their landmarks were found, the cervical,
lumbar and pelvic measurements, and all of them survive editing, calibration, reloading
and export. No new model weights are added.

Global SVA is the horizontal distance from the C7 body centroid, the mean of its four
predicted corners, to the S1 superior-posterior corner, positive when C7 lies anterior.
The overlay draws the C7 plumb line and its offset to S1. In edit mode the C7 centroid and
both S1 endplate corners can be dragged, or moved with Tab and the arrow keys; **Reset**
restores the prediction. Agreement between crops is not an accuracy estimate: review C7's
identity, the regional landmarks and the three SVA handles before accepting the result.
`docs/global-sva.md` describes the construction and the crop search.

## Calibration and export

Without a calibration both SVA values are shown in pixels and marked uncalibrated, and
their millimetre values stay empty. With one they are given in millimetres, and where the
row and column spacing differ the angles use both. Applying or clearing a scale updates the
values at once. The Parameters table and both CSV exports carry the cervical measurements
and separate **C7–S1 SVA (mm)** and **C7–S1 SVA (px)** columns. Femoral-head editing
controls are hidden in cervical and full-spine views.

## Installation

The release provides Windows x64 and macOS Apple Silicon installers plus
`SHA256SUMS`. In-app updating is not included; install the new version over the
existing app. Studies and settings remain in the existing application profile.
The macOS app remains unsigned.
