# Spine Contour 1.0.10

Lateral cervical films can now be measured: a cervical landmark model finds C2 to C7 and
reports the C2–C7 Cobb angle and the C2–C7 sagittal vertical axis. A separate full-spine
workflow combines the cervical and lumbar models to report the global C7–S1 sagittal
vertical axis. Each construction is drawn on the film and its landmarks can be corrected.
Lumbar analysis is unchanged, and this release includes 1.0.9's femoral-head model
unchanged.

## Measuring a cervical film

Select **Cervical** for the study or its workspace folder, then choose whether anterior is
on the image's left or right. The original film is shown first, DICOM included, so the side
can be read before anything runs; the choice is required because the model names
image-left and image-right corners, and the side decides which corner of C7 is posterior.
Studies already in the library stay lumbar unless one is changed deliberately.

Run the model and review the landmarks on the source image. Selecting a measurement row
draws its construction. The six measurement handles can be dragged: the values recalculate
and the corrected points are saved with the study, and **Reset** restores the prediction.
A missing landmark leaves the values that depend on it empty instead of guessing, and a
missed spine detection does not fall back to a whole-image guess.

## The measurements

- **C2–C7 Cobb** is the unsigned acute angle between the C2 and C7 inferior endplates. It
  does not use the C7 superior endplate and carries no lordosis or kyphosis sign.
- **C2–C7 SVA** is the horizontal distance from the C2 body centroid to the C7
  superior-posterior corner, positive when C2 lies anterior on the selected side. The
  plumb line follows the image's vertical; the film is not rotated upright.

Without a calibration SVA is shown in pixels and its millimetre value stays empty. With
one, SVA is given in millimetres, and where the row and column spacing differ the angles
use both. Applying or clearing a scale updates the values at once. Both measurements
appear in the Parameters table and in the CSV exports.

These are model estimates for review. Check that the vertebra marked C2 is C2, that C7 is
fully on the film and that each corner a construction uses sits on visible cortex before
accepting the numbers. `docs/cervical-alignment.md` describes the model, its training data
and the development checks.

## Global C7–S1 SVA

Select **Full spine · HRNET** for a study, or assign **Full spine** to a workspace folder,
for a lateral film that shows both C7 and S1, and choose the anterior side as for a
cervical film. The upper part of the film is searched with the cervical models and the
lower part with the S1 localizer and lumbar HRNET, over overlapping crops; a landmark is
kept only when the crops agree on it, and a missing or disputed landmark is withheld for
review rather than guessed. No new model weights are added.

Global SVA is the horizontal distance from the C7 body centroid, the mean of its four
predicted corners, to the S1 superior-posterior corner, positive when C7 lies anterior.
The overlay draws the C7 plumb line and its offset to S1. In edit mode the C7 centroid and
both S1 endplate corners can be dragged, or moved with Tab and the arrow keys; **Reset**
restores the prediction. Millimetres need a calibration of this image; otherwise the value
is shown in pixels and marked uncalibrated. The Parameters table and both CSV exports carry
separate **C7–S1 SVA (mm)** and **C7–S1 SVA (px)** columns, and cervical values keep their
own. Agreement between crops is not an accuracy estimate: review C7's identity and the
three handles before accepting the result. `docs/global-sva.md` describes the construction
and the crop search.

Femoral-head editing controls are hidden in cervical and full-spine views.

## Installation

The release provides Windows x64 and macOS Apple Silicon installers plus
`SHA256SUMS`. In-app updating is not included; install the new version over the
existing app. Studies and settings remain in the existing application profile.
The macOS app remains unsigned.
