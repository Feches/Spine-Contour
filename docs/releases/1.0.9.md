# 1.0.9 — Femoral-head model update

The femoral U-Net is updated with the best validation-selected candidate from
seven training runs on the expanded annotation set. Native-image CLAHE, a
768-pixel reference canvas resized to 640, horizontal-flip probability averaging,
and a 0.35 threshold are shipped together. Probabilities are restored to source
pixels before thresholding. The ONNX exporter and packaged-model verification use
the new input size; desktop inference remains free of PyTorch.

The annotation snapshot contained 179 positive images (including 26 with only one
head marked) and seven reviewed negatives. Training/validation/test sizes were
126/30/30 images. Unmarked lower regions on one-head cases were ignored during
training and scoring. Two completed heads are not required.

On 29 held-out annotated images, mean Dice against circle-derived labels improved
from 90.52% to 91.25%, and median Dice from 95.32% to 97.19%. This is a modest gain:
three cases remained below 0.80 Dice, one dense head region was missed, and a small
inferior-edge prediction appeared on the one negative test image. There is no
independent BUU test estimate. These are case-disjoint research measurements, not
verified patient-disjoint clinical validation. Existing saved predictions and
manual corrections are retained; re-running inference uses the new model.
