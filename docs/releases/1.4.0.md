# Spine Contour 1.4.0

A level no longer has to be all of its points or none. When only part of a vertebra is on the film, such as C7 with its
inferior endplate hidden by the shoulders, the annotator places the points that are there, finishes the level with
FINISH, and the app measures what that gives. The points that are missing can be placed later, or an endplate removed;
the label record carries the level with null corners where the points are not on the film.

## Placing only some of a level's points

- A vertebral body, C3–C7 and L1–L5, may be finished with one endplate: SA and SP, or IA and IP. C2 may be finished
  with its inferior pair or its centroid alone. T1, S1, T4 and the femoral heads are unchanged.
- While a body or C2 is being placed, the edit bar shows **FINISH** right after FIT (the bar reads FIT, FINISH, UNDO,
  CANCEL, RESET TO PREDICTION, DONE). It is enabled once an endplate, or C2's pair or centroid, is whole and no part is
  half set; its title is `Finish with the points placed; the rest are not on this film`. A level with one corner of a
  pair cannot finish: place the other corner, or UNDO. A level with every point set still finishes by itself.
- The level reads Manual (Edited, when the model had placed it) and Place… is still offered. On the film it draws as
  its endplate line, with the model's whole body dashed beneath it if the other endplate was removed; C2 draws as its
  line, its centroid dot, or both.
- The measurements follow from the points: with C7's superior endplate alone, C2–C7 SVA and C6–C7 segmental lordosis
  read, and C2–C7 Cobb, C6–C7 disc angulation and C7–S1 SVA show —. With C2's centroid alone SVA reads and Cobb and
  C2–C3 show —. With L1's inferior endplate alone the L1–S1 lumbar lordosis and L1PA show —, L1–L2 disc angulation
  reads, and L2–S1 onward read.
- A result from the model with one endplate, which the cervical segmentation can return, now reads Segmented and draws
  as its line, where it read Not placed and drew nothing.
- The edit bar's help line box is narrower, so a long help line wraps inside its box instead of the bar taking a second
  row, in every edit-bar state.

## Completing and removing an endplate

- The chips of a placed body or C2 list every point of its kind, the missing ones with a hollow dot. Clicking a hollow
  chip, or the row's **Place…**, resumes the placement for that endplate: the bar reads `PLACING C7 · 3 OF 4`, the
  placed points are filled in, and the next clicks complete the level. UNDO reaches only the new clicks; CANCEL or
  Escape leaves the level as it was.
- **Delete** or **Backspace** with a point of a body or C2 focused removes that point's endplate (C2's centroid, its
  centroid), committed through the measure queue like a drag. It is refused for the level's last part (CLEAR it or
  uncheck it) and for T1, S1, T4 and the heads, and does nothing while a level is being placed, while the window /
  level panel is open or while the study's run is in flight.
- Outside edit mode a click on a lumbar body's endplate line selects it only for a body with no quadrilateral.

## The label record

- `corners` of a body and of C2 are nullable by endplate: `ia` and `ip` are null for a C7 placed from its superior
  endplate, `sa` and `sp` null together for one placed from its inferior; C2's `corners` is null with a `centroid`, or
  the reverse. At least one part is present and every key is written; `model_corners` has the same shape.
- `edited_points` of a manual entry lists the keys it holds a point for (`["sa", "sp"]`), of an edited entry the keys
  that differ from the model's, a removed endplate's included. A C2 placed from its centroid alone writes only
  `c2_centroid` into the projection and is left out of `vertebrae`.
- The schema id is unchanged, `spinecontour.segmentation-label/1`, with a dated amendment (2026-10-21).
  A second example, `label-record.partial.example.json`, is a cervical film with
  C7's superior endplate alone and C2's centroid alone.
- The backend's lumbar validator (`POST /measure`) accepts a body with one endplate, so a lumbar or full-spine film
  with a partial body measures instead of failing with a 422.

## Verification

1,196 desktop tests (1,161 in 1.3.1; nine cover the level model's partial geometry, FINISH, resuming and removing a
part, seven the projection and the store validator, four the handles and chips, five the canvas, the plates and the
click hit test, five the record and its schema, four the wiring through the real store, and one more in the projection
file). The levels smoke suite, `smoke-levels` (phase dom), read 169/169 on a fresh scratch profile with synthetic films
(154 in 1.3.1; the new checks are section 22: a C2 placed from its centroid and FINISH, a C7 placed from SA and SP with
FINISH disabled after one corner and enabled after two, the row reading Manual with Place… still offered, the chips
`SA SP` filled and `IA IP` hollow, the study measured as cervical with C2–C7 SVA finite and Cobb null, the record with
null corners and `edited_points` `["sa", "sp"]` and `["centroid"]` validating against the amended schema, Edit again
reading it back, the hollow IA chip resuming at `3 OF 4` and two clicks completing C7 with Cobb still null, Delete on
the focused SA removing the superior endplate and nulling SVA, and a second Delete refused). The backend unit suite,
`pytest backend/tests/unit`, read 651 passed and 2 skipped (the partial-measurements file has 147 tests, 139 before:
a body with its superior endplate alone keeps its lordosis, one with its inferior alone gives none and a partial L1 no
L1PA, five rejected shapes, and a full-spine film passing a partial lumbar body through).

Known limitation: the levels dom suite hung intermittently on the development laptop, twice in five runs, once after
section 18 and once after section 16, both around waits that depend on the backend. It never reproduced on demand and
every fresh relaunch passed 169/169. The suite now bounds each call into the page at 90 s and carries a dom-phase
watchdog (120 s without an answer from the page fails the run, cleans up and exits); run it as a bounded PowerShell
job (`Start-Job` with `Wait-Job -Timeout 420`). The other suites and the models are unchanged from 1.3.1 and were not
re-run. These are software checks, not clinical validation. The release workflow builds and tests the Windows and
macOS installers before publication; merging the release PR to `main` publishes v1.4.0.

## Compatibility

The label record keeps its schema id: a record saved before 1.4.0 validates and reads unchanged, and a 1.4.0 record
with a partial level read by a 1.3.x build shows that level as Not placed, nothing else changing. A study whose
geometry holds a partial lumbar body is shown unsegmented, with a console warning, by a 1.3.x build, which is a
degradation and not a corruption. The installer carries the backend with the relaxed lumbar validator. There is no
store version bump, and the bundles and the CSV exports are unchanged. The macOS build remains unsigned.
