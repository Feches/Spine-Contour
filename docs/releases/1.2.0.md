# Spine Contour 1.2.0

This release gives the level editor a comment per film, a status of its own for films placed by hand, a way to undo
one level's edit, and handles that stay out of the way on a zoomed-out film. It also adds a way to carry a set of films
and their annotations to another computer as a folder, and to bring a colleague's annotations of films you already
hold into your own list.

## A comment on the record

- The Vertebral levels panel's footer has a **COMMENT** box. Whatever you type there is saved with **Save edits** into
  the film's label record as `comment`, so the trainer reading the record later knows what you saw: a sacralised L5,
  hardware over a body, a film too dark at T1. A changed comment counts as one unsaved change, so the count, Save
  edits, Discard and the exit prompt all follow it; Discard puts the saved text back.
- Outside edit mode the Analysis screen shows the comment under the actions row, and the Studies search matches it.
  A film's comment travels with its record; a record written before this release simply has none.
- The box keeps its focus, caret and undo history while you type, up to 2,000 characters, and never grows tall enough
  to push Save edits off the panel.

## Hand-placed

- A real film whose result you placed by hand, rather than a model run, now reads **Hand-placed** instead of
  Segmented or Needs review. It sorts after Needs review and before Segmented, counts in TO REVIEW, and **Mark
  reviewed** turns it Reviewed.
- A film whose segmentation failed no longer stays Failed once you place its levels: the first level you commit clears
  the failure, as a successful run would, and the film reads Hand-placed. A later run that fails reads Failed again.
  Taking every level off again (Discard after placing, CLEAR on the only placed level, unchecking it) sends nothing to
  the measurer and returns the film to what the editor found when you pressed Edit: Failed with the failure it held
  then, or Unsegmented when it held none. Any commit in the editor on a hand-placed film clears a failure recorded
  since, a failed re-run's included.
- The Hand-placed pill is Needs review's colour with a hollow dot, the mark that says no model ran.

## REVERT

- A level you have moved reads Edited and its row shows **REVERT**, which returns that one level to the model's
  output, commits it like a drag, and leaves every other level alone. The dashed ghost disappears, the numbers return
  to the prediction's, and Save edits writes the level as `segmented` again with its contour. A femoral head stacks
  REVERT under Place…. Levels placed by hand have nothing to revert to and keep CLEAR.

## The handles

- The selected level's handles were a fixed 6 px at every zoom and covered a whole vertebra on a zoomed-out film. They
  now shrink with the film below 1:1, to a floor of 3 px, their outline and focus ring in step, and keep their size at
  1:1 and when zoomed in. The grab area is unchanged.

## Export and import a folder of annotated films

- On the Find tab, **Export all** (or **Export N selected** when you have ticked rows) writes a **bundle**: a folder
  you choose the place for, named `spine-contour-export-` and the date and time. It holds a manifest and, for each
  film, a folder with the original film file, the study's record, the model's result, your label record (comment
  included) and the manual image scale if you applied one. The rows it takes are the ones the CSV export would take:
  the ticked visible rows, or every visible row when none is ticked. Demo studies are never written. The toast says how
  many studies were written and where, and names any film that could not be found and was left out; such a study is
  still written, without its film.
- On the Studies screen, **Import bundle…** (beside **Choose radiograph**) asks for a bundle folder, works out what
  each film would do and asks first, in the bar: `Import from <folder>: 10 to add, 2 to update, 3 to skip.` with
  **Import** and **Cancel**. Nothing changes until you press Import. A new film becomes a study of its own, ready to
  open, review and edit, with its result, label record, comment, review mark and image scale.
- **Keep the folder where it is.** The imported films stay in the bundle folder; the app keeps no copy of a film. Move
  or delete the folder, or take back the stick it is on, and re-running and windowing need the film found again, and a
  film placed by hand cannot show its original until it is. A model result still opens on its stored image. Copy the
  bundle to a permanent place on the computer first and import from there.
- **A film the library already holds** is recognised by its content, never by its name. Its annotation is replaced
  only when the bundle's label record was saved later than yours, or yours has none; otherwise it is skipped and named
  (`already in the library`), and a film open in edit mode, or with edits left unsaved, is skipped (`being edited`). An
  update keeps your film location, name, subject, timepoint, note and clinical fields. Importing the same bundle twice
  adds nothing.
- **A bundle is for your own machines.** The film inside is the original file, and a DICOM header can hold patient
  identifiers; the study record carries the subject, note and clinical fields you typed. Nothing is uploaded, and the
  app does not remove identifiers. `docs/bundles.md` has what a bundle holds, where each piece goes and what each toast
  says.

## Compatibility

The label record gains an optional `comment` under the same schema id; a record without one validates as before. The
study store gains `labelComment` with no store version bump; a store written by 1.1.1 loads unchanged. A bundle is a new
folder format (`spinecontour.bundle/1`) that changes no stored file; this version refuses a bundle written by a newer
one with a message, and no earlier version can import one. The macOS build remains unsigned.
