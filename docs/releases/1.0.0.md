# Spine Contour v1.0.0

This release promotes Cody's redesigned desktop application and the merged
calibration/accuracy work to `main`, replacing the v0.1.0 application there. It
includes 305 existing commits through `594e63f`, plus the release preparation.
The [complete incoming commit ledger](https://github.com/Feches/Spine-Contour/blob/main/docs/releases/1.0.0-commits.md)
records every imported commit, including the three merges.

## Application and image review

- New landing screen, sidebar, Workspace, Studies, Analysis, Parameters and Settings
  screens; local fonts, updated branding and keyboard-accessible controls.
- A layered radiograph viewer with fit-to-window, cursor-centered zoom, panning,
  measurement constructions and movable labels. The image keeps its aspect ratio.
- Landmark dragging, keyboard nudging, femoral-circle adjustment and retracing,
  reset to the saved prediction, and rerun segmentation. Corrected geometry and
  recalculated measurements save together; failed/pending edits retain the last
  complete saved result and cannot export an inconsistent pair.

## Study library, workspaces and batches

- Saved studies and prediction sidecars, recovery of interrupted/corrupt writes,
  original-file restoration and relocation, individual deletion and Delete all
  studies. Installed builds start without demonstration studies.
- Recursive folder import, editable clinical CSV column mapping, subject/visit/date/
  view fields, per-folder assignments and a clinical-data drawer. WebP is accepted
  alongside DICOM, PNG, JPEG, TIFF and BMP by the picker, drag-and-drop and scans.
- Sequential batch segmentation of selected or visible unsegmented studies, with
  queue counts, Stop, per-study outcomes and protections against deletion/rerun races.
- Searchable, filterable and sortable Parameters grid; CSV export of visible or
  selected studies; paired pre-op/post-op export with independently measured visits,
  explicit duplicate/missing-pair reporting, and deltas over the written values.

## Models, calibration and measurements

- Whole-film/lumbosacral crop selection, U-Net or HRNet vertebral landmarks and saved
  model/crop provenance. Improved fitting of overlapping femoral heads and review
  warnings for clipped heads, weak S1/crop detections and inconsistent geometry.
- Unsupported view labels are excluded from inference. Native 16-bit/float image
  intensities reach preprocessing without 8-bit clipping.
- Automatic OpenCV/Tesseract ruler extraction and DICOM pixel spacing, including
  references outside the lumbar crop. Each study retains its own source-bound scale;
  manual corrections survive reruns and restarts. Missing/uncertain references stay
  uncalibrated while segmentation may continue.
- Anterior, middle and posterior disc heights for L1–L2 through L5–S1 in Measurements
  and ordinary/paired CSV. Anterior/posterior heights connect matching keypoints on
  facing endplates; middle height connects their midpoints. Per-axis spacing is
  applied before distances and per-visit deltas are calculated.

## Installation and versioning

- Windows x64 installer and macOS Apple Silicon disk image, each bundling Python,
  model weights, OpenCV and Tesseract. The macOS app is unsigned; Intel Mac builds
  are not provided.
- `package.json` and the app display v1.0.0. A successful main release builds both
  installers, verifies their source/version and bundled OCR, and publishes one
  numbered `v1.0.0` release with SHA-256 checksums. Numbered tags and published
  binaries are retained; a different source commit requires a version bump.
- The existing `latest-windows` download alias follows the numbered release.
  Preview releases remain separate; PR/manual feature builds publish artifacts only.

## Compatibility and limits

CSV output now includes subject, timepoint and film date, all clinical fields in use,
15 disc-height columns after angular measurements, and per-image calibration fields
when present. Read columns by header name. Missing scales or invalid endplates leave
cells empty. Sacral slope uses `SS`; fabricated zero values are not substituted.

Existing version-1 study libraries remain readable. Production and preview profiles
remain separate; this release does not automatically copy a preview library into
production. An exported measurement CSV is not a workspace-import round trip.

Investigational software; not for clinical use. The checks establish software
behavior, not independently validated anatomical accuracy. Landmark accuracy still
requires review. Unknown radiographic magnification, image-based view classification,
unequal-spacing angular correction, slip measurements and the planned comparison
screen remain outside this release. The three downloaded WebP examples calibrate
successfully but were rejected by anatomical segmentation; they are not a valid
disc-height accuracy benchmark.
